Privacy Policy

English - Mara Dictionary · Last updated 24 October 2026

About this app

English - Mara Dictionary (“the app”) is a free dictionary for English and Mara. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

In short

  • The dictionary works on your phone and does not need an account.
  • Your favorites, search history and settings stay on your phone.
  • The app has no advertising and no tracking tools.
  • The English word you open may be sent to the Datamuse API to get synonyms and antonyms.

What the app uses

Information Why Where it goes
Favorites and search history To show your saved and recent words Stays on your phone
Settings (dark mode, font size, speech rate, colour) To remember your choices Stays on your phone
The English word you opened To get synonyms and antonyms Sent to the Datamuse API (api.datamuse.com)
Microphone (voice search) To turn your speech into a search word Handled by your phone’s speech service

Information stored on your phone

Your favorite words, search history and settings are stored only on your phone. The developer does not receive them. They are removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

Internet use

To show synonyms and antonyms, the app sends the English word you opened to the Datamuse API. The app sends no name, account or other personal information with it. As with any internet request, the service can see your IP address. The results are saved on your phone, so the same word does not need the internet again.

Microphone

If you use voice search, the app asks for permission to use the microphone. Your speech is processed by the speech-recognition service of your phone, which may be provided by Google, depending on your device. The app does not record or store your voice. You can turn the permission off in your phone settings at any time.

Contacting the developer

The Report Error / Contact screen opens your e-mail or WhatsApp app with a message ready for you. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you do, the developer receives what you wrote and your e-mail address or phone number.

Advertising and tracking

The app shows no advertisements and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Web version

The web version needs no sign-in and does not save favorites or history. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep normal server logs.

Dictionary content

The word entries and meanings come from the printed English - Mara Dictionary by P. T. Hlychho and are used with credit to the author.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
